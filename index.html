Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together

What makes your ghostwriting different?

I focus on voice discovery first—understanding your authentic style and then amplifying it. Plus, I provide strategic guidance on content pillars and engagement cadence, not just writing.

How do you measure success?

We track metrics like open rates, CTR, impressions, saves, and comments. But I also focus on qualitative outcomes like brand voice consistency and audience engagement quality.

What's your typical timeline?

Most projects start with a 2-week discovery and strategy phase, followed by content creation. Timeline varies based on project scope, but I'm committed to meeting your deadlines.

Do you work with specific industries?

I've worked across various industries, but my expertise lies in professional services, tech, and education. I'm always excited to learn about new industries and adapt my approach accordingly.
